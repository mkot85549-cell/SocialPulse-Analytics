Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Access

When authenticating via TikTok OAuth, our application accesses public profile data, user ID (open_id), video lists, and comment interactions strictly based on permissions granted by the user.

2. How We Use Information

Data is used solely to generate community analytics, track audience engagement, and process comment management requests.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are securely stored and never shared with third parties. We do not sell or monetize personal user data.

4. Data Deletion

Users can revoke access at any time via TikTok settings. Upon revocation, associated active session tokens are deleted.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data removal requests, contact the developer.
